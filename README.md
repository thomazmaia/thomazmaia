## About Me

I am a professor and researcher at the **Federal Institute of Ceará (IFCE), Maranguape Campus**, working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Software Development, and Research, Development & Innovation (R&D&I)**.

I have been involved in research and technological development projects since 2012, with extensive experience in **industry-oriented R&D projects**, particularly within the **EMBRAPII innovation ecosystem**.

My work focuses on transforming research into practical technological solutions, combining academic knowledge, software development, artificial intelligence, and multidisciplinary project coordination.

I am also the coordinator of **NUVEN**, a research and technological development group focused on **software engineering, artificial intelligence, and industry-oriented R&D**.
